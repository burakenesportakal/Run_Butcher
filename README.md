<h2>🎮 Gameplay Mechanics</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Controls:</strong> WASD to move, ESC for the pause menu.</li>
  <li><strong>Collectibles:</strong> Collect coins 💰 for points and apples 🍎 for extra points and speed boosts ⚡.</li>
  <li><strong>Hazards:</strong> Avoid obstacles 🪨—big rocks shake bridges and trigger fun particles and sound effects ✨.</li>
  <li><strong>Progression:</strong> Checkpoints ⏱️ give extra time, increasing the challenge with every run.</li>
</ul>
